What is Morning Pages?
Morning Pages is a daily bible study app built to cut through the noise of modern technology. This app is built to to make deep intentional Bible study come to life everyday. With just 5-10 minutes, you can get to the heart of scripture and the gospel and grow in your relationship with God.

The team at Sojourners Ministries daily Bible study practice to dive deep into scripture and engage with it personally is Morning Pages.

In a world of that is far too busy and with everything fighting for your attention, we created Morning Pages to help you slow down and think about scripture for yourself. We believe the best way to deepen your relationship with God is to critically think and discover for yourself the riches found within scripture.
